I'm **Haoming (Harvey) Chen**, an undergraduate studying Electrical and Computer Engineering at Rice University, graduating in May 2027.

My research interests broadly lie in **generative modeling**, with a current focus on **flow-based models, flow matching, and diffusion models**. I am interested in how these methods can reconstruct structured data and incorporate the information and constraints available at inference time.

At Rice, I conduct research with **Prof. Santiago Segarra** on generative methods for graph reconstruction and with **Prof. César A. Uribe** on task-based alignment of occupational taxonomies.

> **Seeking PhD opportunities for Fall 2027.** I welcome conversations with prospective advisors about research in generative modeling, flow matching, and diffusion models. Please reach me at [hc80@rice.edu](mailto:hc80@rice.edu).

## Current research

- **Prior-informed graph reconstruction.** Combining structural priors and flow matching to recover graphs from partial observations.
- **Feasible flow matching.** Guiding graph reconstruction toward structural constraints with adaptive primal–dual updates during sampling.
- **Occupation translation.** Comparing task descriptions and task-network structure to align occupational taxonomies and explain proposed matches.
